# Financial Statement & Performance Analysis: Enel S.p.A. vs. Iberdrola S.A. (2023–2024)

## Executive Summary

This repository contains a comparative financial statement analysis and operational benchmark between Enel S.p.A. and Iberdrola S.A., covering fiscal years 2023 and 2024.

The project evaluates how the normalization of wholesale energy commodity prices, strategic asset rotation, and capital expenditures for the energy transition influenced both corporations across margins, capital structures, cash generation, and return metrics.

---

## Methodological Framework

### 1. Financial Statement Restatement
* **Statement of Financial Position:** Analysis of non-current asset rationalization vs. continuous infrastructure expansion, tracking property, plant, and equipment levels exceeding 94 billion euros for each group.
* **Income Statement:** Evaluation of revenue adjustments, operational expenditure optimization, Gross Operating Profit (EBITDA), Operating Profit (EBIT), and capital gains from non-core asset sales.
* **Cash Flow Statement:** Evaluation of operating cash flow resilience, capital expenditure intensity, financing flows, and liquidity buffers.

### 2. Financial Ratio Analysis
* **Profitability:** Return on Equity (ROE), Return on Investment (ROI), and Asset Turnover.
* **Liquidity:** Current Ratio and Quick Ratio movements driven by working capital rebalancing.
* **Solvency & Capital Structure:** Debt-to-Equity ($D/E$), Debt Ratio, Interest Coverage Ratio, and Altman Z-Score.

### 3. DuPont Decomposition Model
A three-tier breakdown to isolate operational efficiency, asset utilization, and financial leverage:

$$
\text{ROE} = \text{Profit Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Financial Leverage}
$$

$$
\text{ROE} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Sales}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Total Sales}}{\text{Average Total Assets}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Average Total Assets}}{\text{Average Shareholders' Equity}}\right)
$$
